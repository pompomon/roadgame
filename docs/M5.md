## 2. Findings

- **L1.** Tests and the autopilot would use the player's save. `SaveService` reads it lazily, so `use_memory()` comes first, from `tests/gut_pre_run.gd` and `--autopilot`; `AudioDirector._ready()` mustn't read it. `run_tests.sh` and `smoke.sh` fail if Godot touches a scratch save.
- **L2.** `plan.md` §3.4's buffer (5 behind, refill from 25 to 50) freed tiles in view. **Δ** 14 behind, refill at 30, 55 ahead, with `PROP_AHEAD + PropLayout.rows_needed()` ≤ `BUFFER_REFILL_AT`; now 20, 37, 55 (docs/ROADGEN.md R6).
- **L3.** Ground drawn per road tile left the screen bare where props stand. **Δ IP M1.6, M4.3, M5.1, `plan.md` §5.3:** a ground band per area, no variants or transition tiles (§5.5).
- **L4.** The art had no contract: `test_area_art.gd` holds it to the grey box's layout.
- **L5.** Nothing checked the save's values; `int(1e20)` is −2^63. `SaveData` checks type and range: a bad value keeps its default and adds to `problems`, one warning. Counts are whole, and counts and distances at most `MAX_NUMBER`, 2^53 − 1, the largest whole number a JSON number holds exactly.
- **L6.** `JSON.parse_string()` logs an error for bad text; `SaveData` uses `JSON.new().parse()`.
- **L7.** `EventBus.disaster_triggered` comes before the pick, so nothing recorded disasters. `DisasterDirector.disaster_started(disaster, area)` comes as it plays one, after the car stopped; `main.gd` relays `EventBus.disaster_started(id, area)`. **Δ IP M5.4:** saved at once, so closing the app mid-disaster keeps it.
- **L8.** The Atlas would credit a run to its last area, and `CarSim` reports an area before the turn behind is judged. `main.gd` calls `GameState.enter_area(id, start_distance, start_turns)` with the distance to the area's first tile in the buffer and the turns before it.
- **L9.** A later version's file is `NEWER`: read as far as known, never written over.
- **L10.** A setting is written 0.5 s after its last change; Android may kill the app sooner. `SaveService.flush()` runs on close, pause, both focus-outs and exit. Of these the Web sends only the window's focus-out, so a change less than 0.5 s before a reload is lost; accepted, as Settings writes when it closes.
- **L11.** On the Web `user://` is IndexedDB, synced after a close, not a rename. `SaveService` writes `save.json.tmp`, closes it, reads it back, renames it and schedules the sync. A corrupt file becomes `save.json.bak`, then the defaults are written; a file that can't be kept is never written over.
- **L12.** Tests found the car as `%Props`' only child; now `WorldRenderer.car` and the test helper's `car()`.

## 5. Design

### 5.1 Files and IDs

Autoloads `SaveService` and `AudioDirector`; classes `SaveData`, `CrossFade`, `MenuScreen` and its screens, `PropDef`, `PropLayout`, `PropPool`, `PropScatter`, `SkidMarks`; directories `audio/`, `assets/`, `tools/art/`, `tools/audio/`. The generators are `SceneTree` scripts run by hand.

### 5.2 Menus

`%Title` (`TitleScreen`) comes after the HUD and game over in `main.tscn`, so it gets Esc first. Its screens extend `MenuScreen`; `TitleScreen.go_back()` returns false on the title itself, so `main.gd` quits. The Atlas shows each area ("???" until reached), the collection each disaster's count and first sighting. Settings: three volumes, Vibration and Screen. `main.gd` starts on the title unless `--autopilot` or `--play`. Every screen fits 360×640.

### 5.3 The save

`user://save.json` holds `version`, `atlas` (per area `discovered`, `best_distance`, `best_turns`), `best`, `disasters` (per id `seen`, `first_area`, `first_distance`, `first_turns`) and `settings`. Distances are in tiles. `SaveData.load_json()` starts from the defaults and returns `LOADED`, `CORRUPT` or `NEWER`. `migrations()` maps each version to its step; `migrate()` reports a missing one with `push_error()`, so each new version needs a step and a test. `SaveService` writes at once on `run_ended` and `disaster_started`, and a setting after `SETTINGS_SAVE_DELAY`. Version 2 adds `tutorial.done` (docs/TUTORIAL.md §5.6).

### 5.4 Audio

`AudioDirector` (`PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS`) cross-fades each area's music and ambience (`CrossFade`, equal power), which go on while paused; the car's sounds pause. `follow(sim)` drives the engine's pitch with speed, and the rumble off the road. `EFFECT_PLAYERS` play the screech, a chime per turn, a thump per `DAMAGE_SOUND_STEP` of health lost, the crash and each disaster (on `disaster_appeared`, docs/M6.md S18). Buses: Music, SFX (reverb in tunnels) and Ambient. Sounds are mono 16-bit 22,050 Hz WAVs, with loop points in a `smpl` chunk.

### 5.5 World art

`assets/palette.gpl` had 32 colours, now 36 (docs/M6.md S9): 8 shared, 4 per area (its ground is its `id_color`) and 4 for effects and UI. Every PNG in `assets/` uses only them, imported lossless without mipmaps. A road sheet has `GreyboxAtlas`'s layout, opaque exactly where its road is. Each area's stretch has a `Sprite2D` band of seamless ground under `Ground`; its 64 px edge, dithered by an 8×8 Bayer matrix, blends over `TRANSITION_TILES`. Without art an area keeps the grey box's. `tests/helpers/art_checks.gd` checks whole images.

### 5.6 Props

A `PropDef` gives a sheet, frames, fps, a foot and a density; an area's densities add up to 1 at most, the chance that a ground cell shows a prop. `PropLayout.row()` fills path tile k's screen row, up to `PROP_SPREAD` tiles either side, off the road and set pieces. Frames fit `PROP_MAX_SIZE` and `PROP_BELOW`, so a prop can reach only nearby road; one that would touch it is left out. `PropScatter` shows row k once `rows_needed()` rows beyond it exist, and hides it when tile k is freed. `PropPool` reuses sprites, which y-sort with the car in `World/Props`. A def that fails `validate()` gets one `push_error()` and never shows.

### 5.7 The car and its skid marks

`car.png` has 17 frames of 36×28, 16 directions and the wreck (`WRECK_FRAME`), shown by the `%Body` `Sprite2D`; the wreck's outline is "tunnel road", so its smoke stands out (`SMOKE_CONTRAST`). While the car brakes for a junction above its target speed, `SkidMarks` leaves a pair every `SKID_SPACING`, `SKID_REAR` behind its centre and `SKID_TRACK` apart, only on the road (R12). They are `Sprite2D`s on `World/Skids`, between the road and the disasters, freed with their tile or oldest first past `SKID_MARKS`.

### 5.8 Font and theme

Pixel Operator 8 (CC0) has an 8 px grid, so the UI uses 8, 16 and 24 px. `ui/theme.tres`, the project theme, is 16 px in palette colours. `make_font.gd` embeds the TTF without antialiasing, hinting or subpixel positioning (L17). It is about 1.6 times as wide as Godot's default (`test_ui_theme.gd`).

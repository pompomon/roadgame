# M4 Areas

Done. The plan (#34) and its stacked PRs, #35–#37, merged on 2026-11-09. The HUD names each area the car enters, each run's first included; the ground blends from one area into the next; the tunnel and the bridge stand over their roads; each area's wrong turn played its own disaster; and `--area=<id>` starts every run in any area. All grey box: M5 brought the art, the props and the audio, and M6 PR 8 (#53) replaced the disasters with legends, which keep §5.8's rules. The full plan, with its verification, negative cases and review history: `git show 08f316f:M4_PLAN.md`.

## 1. Goal and exit criteria

Goal (IP §3): "5 areas, tunnel, bridge and schedule; per-area disaster pools (grey-box art)". Before M4 nothing marked a new area, the set pieces were tinted road, and every pool held M3's sinkhole.

E1–E15 covered the schedule, `--area`, the names, the ground, the set pieces, the disasters, manual checks and CI. Two are cited later:
- **E10.** Each disaster keeps docs/M3.md §5.5's contract, redraws no polygon, doesn't move the car, and with the stop takes 2–4 s from the turn, for any params.
- **E14.** Manual: the Web build in Chrome and Firefox, and the Fold4 (Q6, §7).

## 2. Findings

- **K1.** IP's `validate()` checked only the turns, but `JunctionTrigger` stops judging if the next junction isn't in the buffer (docs/M2.md H31). `AreaSchedule.validate()` rejects an entry whose `gap()`, `set_piece_length` + `max_straight` + 1, is above `BUFFER_REFILL_AT` (25 then, 37 now). The limit keeps a tile spare, so it doesn't rest on `CarSim.step()` moving the buffer on before the trigger looks. The game's gaps are 6–14.
- **K2.** In IP's `Props`, sorted by y and drawn after `RoadLayer`, a set piece couldn't cover the car along its run, or lie under the road: `World/SetPieces` instead (§5.5).
- **K3.** Nothing in M4 would read `AreaDef`'s art, prop and audio fields, `PropDef` or the props stream, so each came with what reads it, in M5 (Q1, Q4).
- **K4.** Nothing reported a run's first area, so the HUD couldn't name it. `main.gd`'s `_on_area_changed()` reports it as it reports each later area (Q2).
- **K5.** IP's occluding roof would hide the car and its crash smoke through a whole tunnel. The tunnel draws over the car, and all of it fades as the car nears, so the car shows through (Q3, §5.6).
- **K6.** A run of set-piece tiles can come in two batches. The renderer adds its scene once the whole run is made and frees it with the run's last tile; `bind()`, on a retry, frees them all.
- **K7.** The dead end of the junction before a run lies `min_straight` + 1 tiles from its centreline, so with `min_straight` 2 other road can come within 2.5 tiles. The river stops `BRIDGE_BANK` (0.5 tiles) short of each end and reaches `RIVER_REACH` (2.0) each way; over 1,010 rivers, other road stayed at least 0.50 tiles off.
- **K8.** `Props`, with the car, draws after `SetPieces` and `DisasterLayer`, so the parts that cover the car have `z_index` 1.
- **K9.** In Godot 4.7.2 each redraw of a `Polygon2D` after its first raises WebGL errors (godotengine/godot#124726, fixed in 4.8), and setting `polygon`, `color` or `offset` redraws it. Points are set once; parts move, scale and fade by transforms and `modulate`, and tests count `draw` signals.
- **K10.** Only the City could start a run, and the Desert is 6.6 minutes in (P2). `AreaSchedule.starting_at(id)` and `--area=<id>` start every run in that area, for tests and manual checks (§5.2).
- **K11.** The autopilot never turns wrong, so IP §9's disasters can't show in its run to turn 230. `tests/sim/test_area_order.gd` checks the areas, and `tests/integration/test_disaster_pools.gd` the pools.
- **K12.** `set_piece` stays a `StringName` id (docs/M1.md G5), not IP's `PackedScene`; `WorldRenderer.set_piece_scenes` maps ids to scenes.
- **K13.** The five areas existed since docs/M1.md G5, and M4 changed only four pools.
- **K14.** `plan.md` §3.7's transition TileSet had no art. An area's first `TRANSITION_TILES` (2) path tiles blend instead (§5.4).
- **K15.** M4 kept M2's path, `CarSim` to `main.gd` to `GameState` and `EventBus.area_changed`, not IP's signal on `GameState`.
- **K16.** IP's 2 s banner had no rule for a pause, a new area or a run's end (§5.3).
- **K17.** `SetPiece.setup(first_tile, length)` drops IP's heading, which the tile has.
- **K18.** The tunnel's `CanvasModulate` and reverb and the river's shader went to M5; the grey box tints the road (`Row.TUNNEL`, `Row.BRIDGE`) (Q4).
- **K19.** docs/M3.md J3 let a disaster change only the car's `scale` and `modulate`; the tornado also spins it, by the root's `rotation`. `CarController` sets the car's position every physics frame and turns only its body. A disaster still mustn't move the car.
- **K20.** Each disaster's script has `static func duration(params)`, the seconds from `play()` to `finished`, which `tests/unit/test_disasters.gd` checks for every disaster in a pool.
- **K21.** The run to turn 230 takes minutes of game time: a sim test, `tests/sim/test_area_order.gd`, not a unit test.

## 5. Design

### 5.1 Files
M4 added `setpieces/` and three disasters, and left `GameState`, `EventBus`, `CarSim` and the road code as they were (K13, K15).

### 5.2 The schedule and `--area`
`AreaSchedule.gap(entry)` and `validate()` apply K1 (`turn 34: the next junction can be 26 tiles away, above BUFFER_REFILL_AT (25)`). `starting_at(id)` returns a schedule from the first entry in area `id`, moved to turn 0 and without its set piece, so a run starts on its own straight; later areas keep their spacing (from Rural: Meadows at turn 66, the Desert at 186). An unknown id is an error, `AreaSchedule: no area <id>`, and returns the schedule itself. `main.gd`'s `_parse_area()` reads `--area=<id>` for every run of the session; `--area=` alone is an error, and runs start in the City. Since docs/ZONE_SELECT.md Z2 the world is built from the whole schedule.

### 5.3 The area's name
`_start_run()` calls `_on_area_changed(_sim.area)` after `GameState.start_run()` (K4), and `CarSim.area_changed` calls it for later areas: it updates `GameState` and emits `EventBus.area_changed`. The HUD's `%AreaBanner` shows the area's `display_name` for `AREA_BANNER_SECONDS` (2 s), counted only while the game isn't `PAUSED`. A new area restarts it, and `run_started` and `run_ended` hide it (K16).

### 5.4 The ground's transitions
An area's first `TRANSITION_TILES` path tiles blend its ground with the last area's: a checker in M4, a band's edge since M5 (docs/M5.md §5.5, `GreyboxAtlas.edge_rows()`). The set-piece tiles belong to the last area, so the blend comes after the tunnel and after the bridge.

### 5.5 Set pieces in the world
`World/SetPieces`, a `Node2D` between `GroundLayer` and `RoadLayer`, doesn't sort by y (K2): the river and the deck lie under the road, and the parts over the car have `z_index` 1 (K8). `WorldRenderer` gathers a run of `SET_PIECE` tiles, adds its scene, gives it the car and calls `setup(run[0], run.size())` (K6). An id with no scene, or a root that isn't a `SetPiece`, is an error, and the run draws as plain road. `@abstract class_name SetPiece` has `car`, `rect` and `heading`; `setup()` calls `_build()`, which sets the polygons' points once (K9).

### 5.6 The tunnel
`Tunnel`, at `z_index` 1, has `FaceX`, `FaceY`, `Mouth` and `Roof`: rock `HALF_WIDTH` (0.55 tiles) each side and `TUNNEL_HEIGHT_PX` (40) tall, its mouth in the face the car enters. Each physics frame `modulate.a` is `opacity(car_distance())`: `TUNNEL_SEE_THROUGH` (0.2) up to `TUNNEL_FADE_NEAR` (0.75 tiles), then `smoothstep` to 1 at `TUNNEL_FADE_FAR` (2.5). At 0.2 a crash's smoke shows through; at 0.35 it didn't.

### 5.7 The bridge
`Bridge` has `River`, `Waves`, `Deck`, `FarRailing` and `NearRailing` (`z_index` 1). K7's river flows across the run. A run too short for the waves between its banks, such as 1 tile, is an error and shows neither river nor waves; the game's bridge is 5 tiles. Nine waves, `WAVE_ROWS` × `WAVES_PER_ROW`, drift by `position` at `WAVE_SPEED` (0.6 tiles/s), spread by `wave_rect()`, and wrap. The railings only show the edge: off the road, M2's capsule test still applies.

### 5.8 Disasters and their pools
`DisasterBase` took the sinkhole's `apply_params()`, which seeds and scales the particles (docs/M3.md J16), and `outline()`, a ground circle of `OUTLINE_POINTS` (32). The contract gained K19 and K20. The director picks from the area's pool with the disaster stream. Since M6 PR 8 each pool is one legend, and `test_disasters.gd` holds every pool to the contract and to 2–4 s.

### 5.9 The tornado, the flood and the quicksand
Retired by M6 PR 8. Timed on physics deltas (docs/M3.md J2), the tornado spun, shrank and faded the car, the flood rose over it, and the quicksand rocked and sank it, each faster when stronger.

### 5.10 Tuning
`config/tuning.gd` keeps `TRANSITION_TILES`, `AREA_BANNER_SECONDS`, `TUNNEL_*`, `BRIDGE_BANK`, `RIVER_REACH` and `WAVE_SPEED`. Tests guard two limits: 2–4 s per disaster (`test_disasters.gd`), and `RIVER_REACH` + `BRIDGE_BANK` at most `min_straight` + 0.5, so a river keeps `BRIDGE_BANK` clear of other road (`test_set_pieces_on_the_road.gd`).

### 5.11 Tests
`test_area_order.gd` drives seed 1 to turn 230 and meets each area and set piece at its turn; `test_set_pieces_on_the_road.gd` drives through both set pieces and checks 1,010 rivers; `test_disaster_pools.gd` takes a wrong exit in each area, started with `--area`, and expects its pool and game over 2–4 s later.

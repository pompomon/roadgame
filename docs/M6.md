# M6 Legends

Done: the plan PR #45 and PRs 1–8 (#46–#53), on 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-12. A wrong turn now ends in a legend of the area, built on a kit of beats, a stage, the car's double, veils and glows. Game over names its fate, the collection tells its lore, and the natural four are gone. The full plan, with its verification, negative cases and review history: `git show 08f316f:M6_PLAN.md`.

## 1. Goal and exit criteria

A wrong turn shows a legend of the area: a 2–4 s scene that keeps the disaster contract, reads at 360×640 and plays the same on Android and the Web. Scary, with no gore (Q8).

E1–E12 cover the pools, the kit, art, sound, words, the natural four, device checks and CI.
- **E6.** No legend flashes, after WCAG 2.3.1: §5.6's test passes on each (S4, S11).
- **E8.** Each legend has its sound, within the effects' peak budget, from the frame it shows (S18), made from its id alone (S17).

## 5. Design

A legend extends `Legend` (`disasters/legend.gd`) and keeps §5.2–§5.10.

### 5.1 Files and IDs

A legend is `disasters/legends/<id>.gd` and `.tscn`. Its id names the rest: its save and `AudioDirector.EFFECTS` key, `assets/legends/<id>/`, `tools/art/legends/<id>.gd`, `assets/audio/sfx/<id>.wav`, `test_<id>.gd` and `Tuning.<ID>_BEATS`. Its chances come from its params alone.

### 5.2 Beats

`Beats` (`disasters/beats.gd`) keeps one clock from `play()`: beat i ends at the delay plus beats 0..i, so the last ends at `duration()`, `params.delay + Beats.total(Tuning.<ID>_BEATS)`. A legend poses its beats on this clock in `_physics_process`, through its `_pose(beat, t)`: no Tween and no timer. A beat that a frame skips still ends: `ended()` lists it, and `_pose(i, 1.0)` comes before the next beat's first pose (N12).

### 5.3 Legend and the stage

- **Hidden until the delay.** Each part is in its scene, hidden by `modulate.a` 0 or `scale` 0. Polygons and particles stay under the legend, and a `Polygon2D`'s `visible`, `color`, `polygon` and `offset` never change (S6).
- **The stage** is World's `%Props`. `play()` puts the `standing` sprites on it, to sort with the props and the car by their foot, and `_exit_tree()` frees them. Only sprites stand: a moved polygon draws again (P10).
- **Each physics frame**, after the camera's (`PHYSICS_PRIORITY` 1), it advances the clock, poses the beats and moves the veil. At the end it calls `push_error()` if the car still shows, and emits `finished` once.

### 5.4 The double

`CarController.double()` is a new Sprite2D drawn as the car's `%Body` is now: offset (-18, -18) and the 36×28 region of its frame. `Legend.take_car()` stages it and hides the car (`modulate.a` 0) in the same frame (N6); the car never moves. By `d` px, it lifts by `offset.y = -18 - d`, keeping its foot (N7), and sinks by cutting its region to `28 - d` high, with `offset.y = -18 + d`. It turns by its region's 16 headings, and shrinks or fades by `scale` and `modulate`. The witch's hex has no double: the car fades in its puff, by its own `modulate.a`.

### 5.5 Veils and glows

- **The veil** is a white `Polygon2D` square of ±`VEIL_HALF_SIZE` (4096 px), `BLEND_MODE_MUL`, at absolute z `VEIL_Z` (2): over the props, the car and the parts (a sky part, such as the UFO's saucer, at `SKY_Z`, 1), under the glows (`GLOW_Z`, 3). Each frame, after the end too, it moves to `camera.global_position + camera.offset`. Hidden by `modulate.a` 0 until the delay, it then darkens over the first beat as `self_modulate` goes from white to `Tuning.VEIL_DUSK` or `VEIL_NIGHT`, never brightening (S4, N8). It is now a `ViewVeil` (`world/view_veil.gd`).
- **A glow** is a Sprite2D, `BLEND_MODE_ADD`, at `GLOW_Z`, tinted by `self_modulate`. Glows add light, so only a legend with a veil has them: over a light ground they clip to white (S10).

### 5.6 No flashing

`Disasters.flashes()` (`tests/helpers/disaster_fixtures.gd`) plays a disaster frame by frame and adds up the glows' light at points 4 px apart, fixed in the World. `test_disasters.gd`'s `test_none_flashes` runs it on every legend, and fails if:
- a point's light rises and falls by more than `FLASH_CHANGE`, a tenth of white's light, more than 3 times in a second;
- its red light, from glows at least 80 % red, rises or falls by a tenth of pure red's more than once;
- a glow covers `FLASH_AREA`, a quarter of 360×640;
- the veil brightens;
- or a `Tuning` `*_HZ` is above 3.

### 5.7 Palette and art

- `assets/palette.gpl` has 36 colours, with Q4's four; `test_area_art.gd` keeps every PNG to them.
- `tools/art/make_legends.gd` runs each `tools/art/legends/<id>.gd`, whose `static func make(dir: String)` draws with `tools/art/legend_art.gd`: named colours, never blended, the same bytes every run, chance seeded by `hash("<id>")`.
- A sheet is one row of whole frames of at most `MAX_FRAME` (96×96), each with its foot at the middle of its bottom row. `test_legend_art.gd` checks them against the scene's sprites (N16).

### 5.8 Fate and lore

The Web has no font fallback, so `test_smoke.gd` checks that the main scene's text, with the disasters' names, fates and lore, has only glyphs of the game's font, Pixel Operator 8. A `fate` has at most 40 characters and shows in `GameOver`'s `%Fate`; a `lore` has 80 and shows in `MenuCard`'s `%Lore`, under `%Details` (P13).

### 5.9 Sound

Each legend's sound is made by `tools/audio/make_audio.gd`'s `_save_legend(id, synth)`, seeded by its id alone (`hash(id)`), so that no other sound changes it (S17). `AudioDirector` plays it at `disaster_appeared`, on the frame the legend shows (S18), so its synth puts each cue at its beat's start, from `Tuning.<ID>_BEATS`.

### 5.10 Anchors

Each list that the legend PRs add to has a comment per area from PR 1, such as `# The City's legends.`: `AudioDirector.EFFECTS`, `make_audio.gd`'s calls and synths, `config/tuning.gd` and `test_disasters.gd`'s pools. A legend adds its lines under its area's comment, a later one after the ★'s, so that stacked PRs' lines never touch.

## 6. Verification

Each PR passed the five checks (`AGENTS.md` §3) and CI; PR 8's head has 141 scripts, 462 tests and 148,384 asserts, and Chrome and Firefox played every legend without errors (#53).

- **P10.** A drawn node moved to another parent draws again; changes of its `modulate`, `z_index` or `position` don't.
- **P13.** Every name, fate and lore has its glyphs in the font. At 360×640 a fate takes two lines at most; the cards overflow the scroll.
- **N6.** `take_car()` doesn't hide the car; **N7.** the lift moves the double's `position`, not `offset`; **N8.** the veil fades by `modulate.a`. Each fails `test_legend.gd`.
- **N11.** `Beats` restarts its clock at each beat: fails `test_beats.gd`.
- **N12.** `Legend` poses only the current beat: fails `test_beats.gd` and `test_legend.gd`.
- **N16.** The sample legend's sheet a pixel wider than its frames: fails `test_legend_art.gd`.
- **N26.** `VEIL_HALF_SIZE` 1024: fails `test_view_veil.gd`'s cover of every screen and zoom.
- **N27.** The veil at z 0, relative, or **N28.** not following the camera: fails `test_legend.gd`.
- **N31.** What a legend leaves behind hides at its end: fails its `test_<id>.gd`.
- **N39.** A polygon hidden, shown, then hidden for good, or **N40.** one made and freed during play: fails `test_none_redraws_a_polygon`.
- **N41.** A legend steps before the camera: fails `test_legend.gd`'s real-frames test.
- **N129.** The change that counts scales with a point's peak: fails `test_pulses_flash_after_glows_add_up_past_white`.
